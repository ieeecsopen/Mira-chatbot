Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is MIRA?
   MIRA is a Sinhala AI Assistant that provides accurate, formal Sinhala responses using Gemini LLM and a RAG pipeline.

2. Does MIRA support Sinhala and English?
   Yes. MIRA supports Sinhala, English, and mixed language (Singlish) input.

3. How does MIRA stay accurate?
   MIRA retrieves relevant information from stored documents using semantic search, then generates grounded answers.

4. Can MIRA understand documents?
   Yes. PDF and text documents can be ingested and used for question answering.

5. Does MIRA store personal data?
   No. MIRA is designed with privacy-first principles and stores no long-term personal user data.

6. What tasks can MIRA perform?
   - Sinhala grammar correction  
   - Sinhala ↔ English translation  
   - Technical explanations  
   - Summaries and rewrites  
   - Project support  
   - Document-based Q&A  

7. Can MIRA be customized?
   Yes. Adding your own documents instantly customizes MIRA’s knowledge.
