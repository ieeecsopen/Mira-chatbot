MIRA is a Sinhala-focused Artificial Intelligence Assistant designed to deliver accurate, formal, and grammar-perfect Sinhala responses. The system combines advanced Large Language Model reasoning with a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline, enabling MIRA to provide highly reliable, document-grounded answers.

MIRA is built to support Sinhala-speaking users across education, business, research, and daily-life assistance. The assistant understands Sinhala, English, and mixed-language inputs, ensuring flexibility for all types of questions.

MIRA uses:
- Gemini LLM for reasoning and natural language generation
- Gemini Embeddings for semantic understanding
- Qdrant vector database for fast retrieval
- A Sinhala grammar polishing layer to ensure professional and correct output

MIRA’s core goals:
- Provide high-quality Sinhala language assistance
- Improve clarity, correctness, and professionalism of Sinhala text
- Enable organizations to build Sinhala-capable AI systems safely
- Support education, technical subjects, and general problem solving

MIRA is modular and extendable, allowing new tools, datasets, and features to be integrated as needed.
